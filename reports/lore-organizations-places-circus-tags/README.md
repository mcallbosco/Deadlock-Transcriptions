# Lore subject assignments

Adds Baxter Society, OSIC, Macomb, Djinn, and The Circus. The Djinn subject includes
Nashala: the newscaster identifies her as a Djinn ambassador, and Mirage explicitly
explains her identity to Rem. The Circus covers Celeste's sideshow and Lenny; the
corpus does not establish a proper troupe name or require a claim that Lenny leads it.

The audit searched every revision's text, reviewed distinct named hits, then
reviewed adjacent conversation turns and supplemental terms (Society, Sandman,
Curator, ambassador, Wyoming, found family, and references to Holliday's home).
Candidates are anchored to exact filenames, revision indexes and expected text.
Assignments propagate through shared audio hashes and exact-text filename lineage
groups. Existing tags and transcript content are preserved.

Excluded: generic acknowledgement turns, ordinary society/government references,
unrelated Alchemy Bureau and Bleak Carnival references, the Coney Island Cyclone
news story, McGinnis's metaphorical flea circus, and ambiguous generated mishearings.
Coherent historical text remains eligible; this is an editorial text audit, not an
exhaustive audio verification. No claims about character identity are inferred
solely from an internal filename.

`candidates.json` records reviewed source revisions and rationale.
`assignments.json` records every resulting change, including propagated aliases.
