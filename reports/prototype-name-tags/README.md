# Spoken prototype names

This tag is about words in the recording's transcript, not internal filenames.
The research began with `config/deadlock/character-mappings.json`, the current
`character-names.json`, and version-specific patron names. These mappings contain
misspellings and ordinary aliases as well as development names, so matching an
alias is not sufficient on its own.

Historical identities were cross-checked against the [Neon Prime history](https://deadlock.wiki/Neon_Prime)
and [Paige's development history](https://deadlock.wiki/Bookwyrm). The local corpus
provides the assignment evidence. It explicitly contains historical proper-name
callouts alongside later names for the same target.

Covered names include Atlas, Forge, Gigawatt, Chrono, Tengu, Nano, Synth, Astro,
Hornet, Orion, Inferno, Sumo, Ghost, Sandeep, Fairfax (Pocket only), Patience,
Digger, Frank, Bookworm, Fencer, Priest, Artist, Chessmaster, Magician, Engineer,
Sapphire Flame and Amber Hand. The latter two have explicit version-name mappings.
Later role-like identifiers are restricted to stock character-target calls rather
than freeform descriptions of an occupation or species.

Excluded examples: Kevin jokes; Viper/Vyper spelling; ordinary patience or forging;
Vindicta described as a ghost; Fairfax Industries, Maximilian, and family/surname
lore; references to orders of the former patrons; generic artist/magician/engineer
prose; speculative Spectre mishearings. Slork/Fathom and Murphy are left out because
current naming and NPC uses make a blanket former-name assignment unsafe. Celeste's
actual unicorn identity is not a prototype-name reference by itself.

Historical generated transcripts are eligible when the name and target agree;
uncertain mismatched-name or garbled references are excluded. This is not an audio
retranscription, and the evidence preserves source authority and all original text.
Shared audio hashes and filename lineage groups receive consistent assignments.
`candidates.json` gives source rationale; `assignments.json` gives the exact changes.
